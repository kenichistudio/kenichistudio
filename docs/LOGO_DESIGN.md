# 🎌 Kenichi Design System

> **"Simple Tools. Powerful Stories."**

This document outlines the visual identity for **Kenichi Studio** and its suite of creative tools. It defines our core philosophy, logo variations, color palettes, and usage guidelines to ensure a consistent, professional, and "strong" brand presence.

---

## 1. Core Philosophy: "Kenichi" (健一)

The name **Kenichi** translates to **"Strong One"** or **"First One"**. Our design language reflects this duality:
*   **Strong**: Bold shapes, high contrast, "Obsidian" dark modes, and "Ember" accents.
*   **First**: Innovation, cutting-edge web technology, and a forward-looking "Neon" aesthetic.

Our tools are not just utilities; they are instruments of creation. The brand feels **engineered**, **precise**, and **essential**.

---

## 2. The Logo Mark: "The K-Cut"

### Visual Construction
*   **The Pillar**: The vertical stroke on the left anchors the design, representing the **Timeline** track.
*   **The Shears**: The two diagonal strokes on the right act as precision **Blades** or **Shears**.
*   **The Cut**: The negative space where they converge mimics the pivot point of a tool, symbolizing the act of editing: *The First Cut.*

The result is an abstract "K" that is instantly recognizable as a creative tool icon.

## 3. Logo Variations System

We have developed a system of 6 core variations to adapt to different contexts (App Icons, Marketing, Dark Mode, Print).

| Variation | Name | Visual Style | Use Case |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **V1** | **Kenichi Blue** | **Gradient BG** (Blue), White Mark | **Primary App Icon** (iOS/Android), Social avatars. The "Standard" face of the brand. |
| **V2** | **Obsidian** | **Black BG**, Gradient Mark (Blue) | **Desktop App** icon, Developer tools, Terminal/CLI branding. Feels "Pro" and technical. |
| **V3** | **Clean** | **White BG**, Black Mark, Drop Shadow | **Documents**, Press Kits, minimal environments where color might clash. |
| **V4** | **Ember Inset** | **Pale Red Gradient**, White Mark (Inset) | **Release/Beta** builds, "Live" recording status, or specific micro-apps (e.g., Kenichi Recorder). |
| **V5** | **High Levitation** | **White BG**, Blue Mark, Deep Shadow | **MacOS** aesthetic, modern web landing pages. Feels airy and lightweight. |
| **V6** | **Midnight Levitation** | **White BG**, Black Mark, Deep Shadow | **High Contrast** minimalism. A variation of V5 for when blue is too playful. |

> **🎨 Asset Location**: All SVG source files and variations are located in `assets/logos/`.

## 4. Color Palette

### Primary Colors
*   **Kenichi Blue**: A vibrant, electric gradient representing creativity and flow.
    *   Start: `#3B82F6` (Blue 500)
    *   End: `#1D4ED8` (Blue 700)
*   **Obsidian Void**: A rich, deep black for pro-level interfaces.
    *   Hex: `#09090b` (Zinc 950)
*   **Paper White**: Pure white for canvas and "levitating" effects.
    *   Hex: `#FFFFFF`

### Accent Colors
*   **Ember Red**: Used in V4 and for destructive actions/recording.
    *   Start: `#FECaca` (Red 200) -> End: `#DC2626` (Red 600)
*   **Electric Yellow**: (Planned) For "Selection" highlight states and "Sparks" micro-apps.

---

## 5. Typography

**Primary Font: Outfit**
*   **Why**: A geometric sans-serif that perfectly mirrors the constructed nature of the logo. It is modern, legible, and has "tech" DNA without being cold.
*   **Weights**:
    *   **Bold (700/800)**: Headlines and Brand Name.
    *   **Regular (400)**: UI Text and body copy.

---

## 6. Usage Guidelines

### Do ✅
*   Use **V1 (Blue)** for the main "Kenichi Studio" web application link.
*   Use **V2 (Obsidian)** for the "Kenichi Desktop" downloadable application.
*   Use the **Gradient Logo** text style on dark backgrounds.
*   Ensure the "K-Cut" mark always has sufficient breathing room (clear space).

### Don't ❌
*   Do not rotate the logo. The "Pillar" must remain vertical.
*   Do not change the angle of the "Shears".
*   Do not use the logo in low-contrast combinations (e.g., Blue mark on Black BG is okay, but Blue mark on Blue BG is forbidden).
